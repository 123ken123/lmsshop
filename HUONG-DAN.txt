CAU TRUC THU MUC (phai dung nhu vay):
  shop\
    server.js
    products.json
    start.bat
    them-key.ps1
    public\
      index.html     <- file giao dien (da doi ten tu index_1.html)

CHAY:
  1. Mo start.bat bang Notepad, doi WEBHOOK_KEY va ADMIN_TOKEN, luu lai.
  2. Double-click start.bat. Thay "Shop chay tai http://localhost:3000" la OK.
  3. Mo trinh duyet: http://localhost:3000
  4. Them key vao kho (neu khong san pham se hien HET HANG):
       .\them-key.ps1 -ProductId 1 -Keys "KEY1","KEY2" -Token "token-admin-cua-ban"
     ProductId 1-4 theo products.json.
  5. Khi len mang: dat WEBHOOK_KEY trong SePay giong trong start.bat,
     URL webhook: https://ten-mien/api/webhook/sepay
